    அனைத்து 108 பலன்கள் · All 108 readings

    About this chakram

    Sahadeva, the youngest Pandava, is remembered in Tamil tradition as a master of astrology. This 108-square chakram is a prasna (horary) method: the answer belongs to the moment you ask, not to your birth chart.

    The English translations and the favourable, mixed or unfavourable tags are summaries added for this page; they are not part of the tradition.

    Tradition advises asking only on matters that truly matter, and not repeating the same question soon after. Treat the answer as guidance for reflection, not as a certainty.
